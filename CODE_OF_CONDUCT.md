# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors, maintainers, and users of **WASS2S**, we are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, and respectful community. We pledge to make participation in the WASS2S community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to building a collaborative environment that values scientific integrity, openness, reproducibility, diversity, and mutual respect.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and welcoming to others.
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Sharing knowledge openly and encouraging collaboration.
- Focusing discussions on scientific evidence, reproducibility, and the improvement of WASS2S.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Personal, political, or discriminatory attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or collaborative work.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or scientific community.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, pull requests, issues, discussions, documentation, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. When appropriate, maintainers will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all WASS2S community spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues
- Pull requests
- GitHub Discussions
- Documentation
- Tutorials and notebooks
- Workshops, training sessions, and conferences related to WASS2S
- Any online or offline event where an individual is representing the WASS2S project

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

Reports may be submitted by opening a private GitHub discussion or by contacting: @hmandela

All reports will be handled promptly, fairly, and confidentially. Project maintainers are committed to respecting the privacy and security of everyone involved.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines when determining the appropriate response to violations of this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**

Use of inappropriate language or other behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**

A private written warning explaining the nature of the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested where appropriate.

---

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**

A violation through a single incident or repeated inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**

A formal warning with clear expectations for future conduct. Continued violations may result in temporary restrictions on participation.

---

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**

A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**

A temporary suspension from participating in WASS2S community spaces and events for a specified period.

---

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**

A pattern of repeated violations, harassment, intimidation, or aggressive behavior toward members of the community.

**Consequence**

Permanent removal from participation in all WASS2S community spaces.
